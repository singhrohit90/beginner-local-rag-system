## Phase Goal

**As a** chat user, **I want to** ask a follow-up inside a conversation that the server remembers for me, **so that** the answer uses my earlier turns while my history stays private to me, disappears with its document, and fits the model's context window.

RESEARCH "Key insight": every defence already exists as a pattern; the work is ordering (what is read after the lease, what is checked under the store lock).
