## Authoring recipe (the executor follows it row by row)

- **Converted drafts:** when `data/golden/conversations_draft.jsonl` exists (plan 03-03), each of its rows `c001` to `c008` keeps its id, category, turns and message; `expect` becomes `answer` (or stays `clarify`); `key_facts` and `evidence_terms` are copied from the row named by its `source_id`; `notes` records `converted from the Phase 3 draft`. The draft file stays in place and is read by no code. When it is missing (RESEARCH A7), author every row from the seeds and number from `c001`.
- **Sources:** every answer row names one answerable `source_id` from `data/golden/ddia_questions.jsonl` and copies its `gold_pages`, `key_facts` and `evidence_terms` verbatim; no source id is used twice in the file. Seeds (RESEARCH "Authoring seeds"): pronoun q015, q019, q021, q026, q027, q016, q031, q034, q022, q023; constraint q041, q029, q003, q012, q011, q013; standalone q002, q017, q028, q030, q036, q042, q008 and one more; topic switch: sources from other chapters than the earlier turns; any answerable id not used elsewhere may replace a seed.
- **pronoun_follow_up:** `standalone` = the source question verbatim; the earlier turn names the subject; `message` elides it with a referring word (it, its, that, this, they, them, these, those, one, other) so the standalone's distinguishing words are missing from the message; at least 3 rows add a second turn naming a more recent distractor entity.
- **constraint_follow_up:** `standalone` = the source question verbatim; `message` keeps a constraint the source question itself contains (a number such as 64-bit, a table or example name, a version such as SQL:1999, a quoted term) and elides the subject; `must_contain` lists those constraint terms, each present in both the message and the standalone (the loader checks this).
- **topic_switch:** the earlier turns are on another chapter; `message` = `standalone` (the source question, or a shorter wording of the same question that keeps its key noun phrases, noted as `shortened from qNNN`); `must_not_contain` = at least one content word of the earlier turns that the standalone does not contain.
- **standalone_in_conversation:** like topic switch but the earlier turns may be on any topic; the message is already complete; at least half of these rows (and of the topic-switch rows) are under 10 words or carry a marker word (Pitfall 2).
- **ambiguous_follow_up:** two turns name two candidates equally (for example leader-based and leaderless replication; B-trees and LSM-trees; document-partitioned and term-partitioned indexes; two-phase and three-phase commit); `message` asks which one without naming either; `expect` `clarify`; `standalone` null; `gold_pages`, `key_facts`, `evidence_terms` empty; `must_contain` = the two candidate terms as they appear in the turns.
- **Every row:** turn questions and answers within the loader's word caps (`HISTORY_QUESTION_MAX_WORDS`, `HISTORY_ANSWER_MAX_WORDS`), answers true to the book; no URL, email address, IP address, `[S n]` marker or instruction-like text (`data/golden/question_writer_rules.md`); `"review": "pending-human"`.

## Decisions recorded in this plan

- **Size (D-02 and the orchestrator resolution):** 30 answer rows plus 4 clarify rows, so n = 30 and the band is 10.0 points (a gain needs net wins of at least 4). Growing the set past about 30 turns is deferred (CONTEXT Deferred Ideas).
- **One row per source question** (RESEARCH statistics point 6) so paired items are not correlated through a shared source.
- **Shortened standalone wording** is allowed only for topic-switch and standalone rows, only to put the message under 10 words (Pitfall 2), and is recorded in `notes`; the owner reviews it with every other row.
- **Rewording after the check:** at most two rounds, only the `message` of pronoun and constraint rows (or a replacement row from an unused source), only on off/oracle results; never after any rewriter output.
