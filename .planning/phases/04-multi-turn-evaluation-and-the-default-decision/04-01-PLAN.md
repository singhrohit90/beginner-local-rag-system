## Decisions recorded in this plan

- **Golden schema (orchestrator resolution, refines D-01):** `evidence_terms` is required on answer rows so the experiment always scores with `strict_trace`; `source_id` and `notes` are optional provenance; `standalone` is `Optional[str]` and null only on clarify rows; `expect` is `answer` or `clarify`, and the Phase 3 draft's `rewrite`, `new_topic` and `standalone` values become `answer` with the category carrying the distinction. Rated costly (D-01): later experiments and docs read this schema; the loader is the single place it is defined.
- **Unknown keys:** checked explicitly against `REQUIRED_KEYS` and `OPTIONAL_KEYS` and reported as `ValueError` with `path:line` and the key names (RESEARCH "Schema reconciliation"), instead of relying on the dataclass `TypeError` the way `load_golden` does.
- **Turn word caps:** a turn question over `HISTORY_QUESTION_MAX_WORDS` (60) or an answer over `HISTORY_ANSWER_MAX_WORDS` (40) words is rejected rather than warned about, so what `render_block` shows the rewriter equals what was authored (RESEARCH recommendation, stricter end chosen).
- **normalise:** lower-case, collapse whitespace, strip, then drop trailing `?`, `.` and `!`; used for the standalone-equals-message rules and `expects_rewrite`.
