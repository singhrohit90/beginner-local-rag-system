## Decisions recorded in this plan (Claude's discretion)

- **concat join:** previous user question, one space, then the message (`item.turns[-1].question + " " + item.message`).
- **Clarify items and retrieval:** they have no gold pages, so `off`, `oracle` and `concat` are `None` for them and they never enter a hit@5 mean (PITFALLS Pitfall 11).
- **Effective query:** `result.question` for every status except `clarify` (which retrieves nothing); for `fallback`, `standalone`, `new_topic` and `skipped` that equals the raw message, which is what the service does (Phase 3 truths).
- **Regression on-arm retrieval** runs the pipeline a second time on the effective question rather than copying `off`, so identical chunk ids also prove retrieval is deterministic.
- **Rule guard scope:** the refusal applies to any mode that can call the rewriter (the `on` arm and `--regression-only`); `--check-set` and `--check-rule` need no guard.
- **rule_commit:** the oldest commit that added the file (`git log --diff-filter=A --format=%H -- docs/chat_memory_eval.md`, last line).
